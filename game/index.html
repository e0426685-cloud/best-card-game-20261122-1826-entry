EMBER DUEL

Four suits. One ember. Zero mercy.

You and Bramble the Thorn Warden each hold 30 life and a deck of 24 cards. Spend ember to play cards: Flame burns, Tide mends, Thorn shields, Stone hoards ember. Play two cards of the same suit in one turn and they resonate for +2 power. Reduce Bramble to 0 life before yours runs out.

▲ Flame · damage ● Tide · healing ◆ Thorn · shield ■ Stone · ember

Mouse: click cards · Keyboard: 1–5 play a card, Enter ends turn, N new duel

VICTORY

Press R to duel again

HOW TO PLAY

Each turn you gain ember (up to 10) and draw a card. Click a card — or press its number key — to play it if you can afford its ember cost. Cards resolve at once: damage hits the enemy's shield first, then life. Healing restores life, shield blocks damage, ember cards refill your ember for bigger plays next turn.

Resonance: the second card you play of the same suit in one turn gains +2 to its number. Chain suits to win big.

Bramble plays after you end your turn. First to 0 life loses.
